Planetary Guardians · Studio.One · NYC Climate Week 2026

Planetary Health Club

Health clubs are for the few. We opened one for all 8.3 billion of us: a free, satirical wellness club on East 40th Street showing how closely the planet's health and our own are tied together.

ClientPlanetary Guardians
AgencyStudio.One
WhenSeptember 21–22, 2026
My rolePartner

The idea

New York is full of luxury health clubs selling botox, cryotherapy and treatments most people can't afford. We borrowed the format and flipped it. The Planetary Health Club was free, open to everyone, and every "treatment" pointed back to the health of the planet.

The joke lands first. The science follows.

The staff

Brand ambassadors in custom scrubs carrying the Planetary Health Check symbol guided visitors through the club. Subject matter experts in custom lab coats explained the science behind every message.

Zone 01

Toxic Positivity Juice Bar

Visitors arrived at a juice bar selling hidden environmental risks as wellness products.

Zone 02

Sauna of Truth

A dark, red-lit room kept uncomfortably warm, with a 60-second film. Sixty seconds, because every minute someone dies from heat-related causes.

Zone 03

Treatment Center

iPad stations with the remedies: the Planetary Health Check 2026, live Climate TRACE pollution data and the partners doing the work. It ended with a letter to the UN.

The Sauna of Truth · the film that played in Zone 02. Turn the sound on.1:29

The menu

Forever FormulaPacked with PFAS, for that everlasting effect.
VitaMinimizerMade with zero pollinator-dependent produce. 100% bee-free.
Protox WaterComes with a hint of waste: fertilizer run-off, nitrates and phosphorus.

The final station

Visitors signed Planetary Guardians' letter to the Presidents of the UN Security Council and General Assembly, asking for an annual Planetary Science and Security briefing and for nations to appoint Chief Planetary Scientists.

The take-home

Every guest left with a Planetary Treatment Regimen printed on seed paper, pointing them to the Planetary Health Check, the Renewable Freedom report and the partners. Once read, it goes back in the ground and grows pollinator-friendly plants.

Coral Gardeners · Studio.One · 2026

Power to the Gardeners

A ground-up rebrand for Coral Gardeners, the reef restoration movement started by French Polynesian island kids. New vision, mission and values, a new identity, and a reset for the organization's next chapter.

ClientCoral Gardeners
AgencyStudio.One
ScopeStrategy, identity, guidelines, web
My rolePartner

The brief

Coral Gardeners started in 2017 when a group of island kids watched their home reef dying and decided to act. It had grown into an organization with a global following, labs and branches across the Pacific, but its brand still looked and sounded like the early days. The job was to define what the organization stands for now, and build a brand that could carry it into a new chapter.

The idea

Conservation is usually told as doom and gloom, led from boardrooms. Coral Gardeners is the opposite: surf bums, fish nerds, scientists and island kids, growing the reef back by hand.

So we built the brand on that: Power to the Gardeners. A rallying cry that says the solutions are in people's hands, and an invitation for anyone to become a gardener.

Brand foundation
VisionA world revived. From the reef up.
MissionGive people the power to garden the world back to life.
PlatformPower to the Gardeners.
Tenets
Rebellious TogetherBold · Raw · Confident
Rigorously ObsessedPassionate · Purpose-driven · Expert
Infectious OptimistsRelatable · Vibrant · Inviting
Wild MakersProgressive · Imaginative · Experimental
Power to the Gardeners · the brand film. Concept and script by Studio.One.1:07

The voice

Punchy, inspiring, grounded and open. No guilt trips and no jargon, just lines a gardener would actually say: Plant what you preach. Beat the odds, coral by coral. Grow it all back.

The identity

The new symbol keeps the legacy coral logo but rebuilds it entirely from overlapping circles, so a coral structure emerges from a network of connected parts: the gardeners. The palette is deliberately restrained. Black and white let the ocean photography lead, with Rally Red for urgency and Pelagic Blue for depth.

PagerDuty · Studio.One · 2026

Make Sure

A new brand platform and campaign for PagerDuty, built for a market where AI is making every system faster, more complex and more likely to break.

ClientPagerDuty
AgencyStudio.One
ChannelsCTV, OOH, digital, social
My rolePartner

The brief

Almost everyone in IT knows PagerDuty, but the product had outgrown how people saw it. The market still filed it under "on-call," while competitors told bigger stories about observability, automation and AI. The problem wasn't awareness. It was relevance.

The insight

AI is accelerating complexity, creating as much risk as capability. In that world, PagerDuty's oldest strength becomes its most valuable one: trust that systems will work when it matters.

The idea

Make Sure. Two words that say what PagerDuty does and what customers are really buying: confidence. Instead of chasing the AI narrative, the platform extends the trust PagerDuty already owns, from fixing incidents to preventing them.

It's built as a system, not a slogan. "Make Sure" leads and the second line flexes for every audience, from executives to developers.

Make Sure · the CTV spot0:51
The system

One line, endless endings

"Make Sure" stays fixed. The second line changes to fit the channel, the audience and the moment.

  • Make Sure it works.
  • Make Sure one incident doesn't become ten.
  • Make Sure small issues don't become big jobs.
  • Make Sure you catch it before you ship it.
  • Make Sure progress doesn't create problems.
  • Make Sure you call your mother.

  • Digital & social
  • Campaign toolkit

The toolkit

We also built a campaign toolkit so PagerDuty's in-house team could roll "Make Sure" out across every channel themselves, keeping it consistent long after launch.
